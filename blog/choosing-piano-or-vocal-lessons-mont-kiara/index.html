Piano or Vocal Lessons for Kids near Mont Kiara

16 Oct 2026Master ArtistMusicParents

Near Mont Kiara, the music lesson you can book now is one to one with Sabrina Hew. She teaches music and voice at Master Artist in Desa Sri Hartamas. The fee is RM80 to RM100 per student per hour. There is no group class. There is no timetable yet. Enquire to arrange a time.

This note is for parents comparing singing with playing. The fee above is the price on our prices page, for individual lessons with Sabrina. Piano is not listed until a teacher is hired, so this article does not give a piano timetable or a piano fee.

Where the studio is, if you live near Mont Kiara

The studio is at 38-2, Jalan 28/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur, upstairs above Savor. Hartamas is a short drive from Mont Kiara, which is why many families come from one or the other. The full address, map and opening hours are on the visit page. The studio is closed on Saturday.

Music times are not on a public grid. You enquire, and we arrange a lesson with Sabrina that fits the student. Do not expect a fixed after-school slot on the website. There is not one.

Choose voice if your child already sings

Music and voice lessons are for a child who sings in the car, at home, or at school, and for a child who is shy about it and wants a room where singing is the point. Sabrina Hew, the founder, teaches the lesson herself. It is one student and one teacher. The work is breath, pitch, tone and the confidence to sing a song they chose, not a copy of someone else’s recording.

The fee is RM80 to RM100 per student per hour. That range is the whole price. It is not a monthly pack, and it is not a group rate. If two children both want to learn, each one has their own lesson. We do not run a group voice class.

A child does not need to read music before the first lesson. They do need to stay in the room and try a sound more than once. Older beginners are welcome, including teenagers who only sing with headphones on. Adults can enquire too. The same hourly fee applies. There is no separate adult rate.

If they want an instrument in their hands

Some children head for any keyboard in the house. Some want to hold an instrument. That interest is real. At the moment we do not list piano, because a piano teacher is not hired yet. We will not invent a day, a class size or a second teacher to fill the gap.

Until that changes, the lesson you can arrange is still the one with Sabrina: music and voice, one to one, RM80 to RM100 an hour. A child who loves songs can start there. A child who only wants piano can wait, or you can ask us when you enquire and we will say plainly if piano is still paused.

Listening at home still helps either way. Singing, clapping and playing with music do not need a lesson first. If you later add piano, we will tell you what is useful at home after a teacher is actually teaching it, not before.

What the RM50 trial is, and what it is not

The RM50 trial is for weekly art classes. It is credited in full against the first month if you continue with art. It is not a trial for music, and it is not a trial for voice. Please do not use the art trial form to book a singing lesson.

For music, enquire to arrange. Tell us the student’s name, age and which days you can do. We will reply with a time if Sabrina has one. Saturday is not a lesson day. The studio is shut.

Weekly art, if that is what you wanted instead, is RM320 a month. That is RM80 for a 2-hour class, or RM840 for a 12-class term. Those art prices are on the same prices page. They are a different product from the hourly music lesson.

A simple way to decide

Listen for a week before you write to us. If your child sings when they think nobody is scoring them, enquire about voice with Sabrina. If they only want to play, say that in the message. We will not place them in a group, because there is no group to place them in.

You do not need an instrument at home before you enquire. For voice, the instrument is the child. What helps at home is a few quiet minutes to sing the thing they learned, not an app and not an exam book.

PBS KIDS for Parents makes a similar point for younger children: singing, clapping and playing with music at home are enough before formal lessons. Our line for structured lessons is the one we can actually teach. That is one to one with Sabrina Hew, at RM80 to RM100 per hour.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a music or voice lesson?

Lessons with Sabrina Hew are RM80 to RM100 per student per hour. The lesson is one to one. There is no group voice class and no monthly group fee.

Is there a timetable?

Not yet. Enquire to arrange a time. We will not post a grid of days until there is one. Saturday the studio is closed.

Can two children share a lesson?

No. Each student has their own hour with Sabrina. A shared lesson is a group class, and we do not offer one.

Can we use the RM50 trial for singing?

No. The RM50 trial is for weekly art classes, and it is credited against the first art month if you continue. For music, enquire to arrange a lesson.

Bring one clear question when you enquire: singing now, or waiting for piano. You will get a straighter answer than another week of comparing old price lists. We are at 38-2, Jalan 28/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas.
