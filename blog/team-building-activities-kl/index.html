Team building activities in KL

26 Nov 2026Master ArtistArtAdultsBaking

Team building activities in KL at Master Artist are workshops you do together at the studio in Desa Sri Hartamas. You paint, pour candles, crochet or bake, and each person takes a finished piece home the same day. A public seat is one place on a listed date. A private team day is a different booking, with its own package.

Team building activities in KL

These are the workshops we run. Each one is two hours. A drink and all materials are included. You do not bring paints, wax, yarn or ingredients. Beginners and people who already paint, crochet or bake book the same seat.

If you searched for a sip and paint, a painting workshop is that kind of evening: one guided canvas, with a drink included. It is a booked seat, not a walk-in. Public dates are on the workshops page. A team day is not those seat prices added together.

  • Acrylic canvas. RM89, or RM160 for a pair. One brush-on canvas, 30x40cm, from a guided theme. Up to 18 seats, in the L2 front room.
  • Textured and impasto canvas. RM89, or RM160 for a pair. One canvas with texture paste or thick impasto paint and palette knives, from a guided theme. Up to 20 seats, in the L2 front room.
  • Glow-in-the-dark canvas. RM85, or RM153 for a pair. One canvas in glow acrylics. It looks finished in daylight and lights up when the lights go off. Up to 18 seats, in the L2 front room.
  • Watercolour. RM80, or RM144 for a pair. One watercolour on cotton paper. Themes are botanicals, fruit or koi. Up to 20 seats, in the L2 front room.
  • Candle making. RM89, or RM160 for a pair. Pour, scent and decorate candles in gift-ready jars, from a seasonal theme, boxed to take home. Up to 16 seats, in the L1 front room at Savor.
  • Chunky crochet. RM95, or RM171 for a pair. Basic stitches with chunky yarn and a big hook, and a small bag to take home. No experience needed. Up to 20 seats, in the L2 front room.
  • Bake Lab. RM95, or RM171 for a pair. Specialty breads and cakes are RM85. One bake, boxed to take home: focaccia, pizza dough, cinnamon rolls, bagels or milk bread. Up to 24 seats, or 20 for a specialty bake, in the L1 kitchen.

Three dates are on the calendar now. Acrylic, glow, watercolour, candle making and chunky crochet have no public date yet.

  • Wednesday 25 November, 7pm–9pm — Sunny Bloom: Impasto Sunflower. Textured canvas, RM89, or RM160 for a pair, in the L2 front room.
  • Thursday 26 November, 7:30pm–9:30pm — Golden Impasto Sky. The same format and price, in the L2 front room.
  • Sunday 29 November, 3pm–5pm — Gingerbread Baking Workshop at Savor Level 1. RM160 a person, or RM310 for two. Seats 20. You bake a gingerbread man and decorate a gingerbread house. The usual Bake Lab price of RM95 is not the price of this date.

Sunday workshops do not start before 12pm. Gingerbread starts at 3pm. Book on the date page before you arrive.

What is included, and how many can come

A public seat includes a drink and all the materials. You take the piece home the same day. Please arrive 15 minutes early. We are upstairs, above Savor.

Seats are prepaid and non-refundable. We confirm on WhatsApp and send payment details there. A reschedule is free within 24 hours of booking. After that it is RM10 a person, with 48 hours' notice. Each session needs a minimum of six people. If a date does not reach six, we message you and offer another date.

You can add a meal at Savor downstairs, from RM25. You choose from the menu and pay online. An evening session has the meal before. A daytime session has it after. Sunny Bloom and Golden Impasto Sky are before. Gingerbread, at 3pm, is after. These are Launch Season prices, until 30 April 2027. A public holiday session is 10% more, except Sunday 8 November.

A private team day

A public seat is one chair at the workshops above. A private group is booked on the groups page. An art team day is RM1,200 for up to 10 people. A baking team day is RM1,700 for up to 15. A mixed team day, with two or three streams in parallel, is RM2,400 for up to 20. Each extra person is RM100. The price includes materials and a teacher for each stream.

Team days run from 6 people. Up to four rooms can run side by side: the L2 front room, the L1 front room, the L1 kitchen and the L2 recording studio. Everyone takes something home. A dinner and evening workshop is RM150 a person, from 10 people. Friday team days finish by 12:30pm. Saturday is closed.

Send the package, the numbers and a date from the groups page. We confirm the plan, then send payment details on WhatsApp. Seats are prepaid. A birthday is a different booking. Use the birthdays page for that, rather than taking public seats.

Team building near me in KL

For team building near me in KL, the studio is upstairs, above Savor, at 38-2, Jalan 28/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur. Hartamas is a short drive from Mont Kiara. Painting uses the L2 front room. Candle making uses the L1 front room at Savor. Bake Lab uses the L1 kitchen. Gingerbread is at Savor Level 1.

Hours are Sunday 10am–6pm, Monday to Thursday 10am–10pm, and Friday 10am–5pm. Saturday is closed. On Fridays there are no workshops after 12:30pm. On Sundays there are no workshops before 12pm.

Sunny Bloom is on the 25 November page. Golden Impasto Sky is on the 26 November page. Gingerbread is on the 29 November page. For any other format, start on the workshops page. You can also WhatsApp +60 10-277 9426 or email info@masterartist.co and name the session. For a private team, use the groups page.

Frequently asked questions

What team building activities can we do in KL?

Team building activities in KL at the studio are painting, candle making, crochet and Bake Lab. Watercolour is RM80. Acrylic and textured canvas are RM89. Glow canvas is RM85. Candles are RM89. Crochet and Bake Lab are RM95. Each one is two hours. A drink and all materials are included, and you take the piece home the same day.

How much does a team day cost?

An art team day is RM1,200 for up to 10 people. A baking team day is RM1,700 for up to 15. A mixed team day, with two or three streams in parallel, is RM2,400 for up to 20. Each extra person is RM100. Prices include materials and a teacher for each stream.

How many people can come?

Team days run from 6 people. Art covers up to 10, baking up to 15, and a mixed day up to 20. Each extra person is RM100. A dinner and evening workshop is RM150 a person, from 10 people. Candle making seats 16. Acrylic and glow seat 18. Crochet, textured canvas and watercolour seat 20. Bake Lab seats 24.

Where is team building near me in KL?

For team building near me in KL, the studio is upstairs, above Savor, at 38-2, Jalan 28/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur. It is a short drive from Mont Kiara. Saturday is closed. Friday team days finish by 12:30pm. Sunday workshops do not start before 12pm.

Is a public workshop the same as a team day?

No. A public seat is one place on a listed date, at that workshop's price. A team day is a private booking. Art is RM1,200 for up to 10, baking is RM1,700 for up to 15, and a mixed day is RM2,400 for up to 20. Each extra person is RM100. Materials and a teacher are included.

If you want team building activities in KL, book a public seat on the workshops page, or ask for a team day on the groups page. Message us on WhatsApp when you are ready and we will confirm the plan.
