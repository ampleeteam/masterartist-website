Creative Workshops KL This Week

25 Nov 2026Master ArtistWorkshopsAdultsBaking

Creative workshops in KL this week, at Master Artist in Desa Sri Hartamas, are three booked sessions. Most are two hours, and Gingerbread runs two and a half. Wednesday 25 November, 7pm–9pm, is Sunny Bloom, a textured canvas at RM89. Thursday 26 November, 7:30pm–9:30pm, is Golden Impasto Sky, also RM89. Sunday 29 November, 3pm–5:30pm, is a gingerbread baking workshop at RM160. Materials and a drink are included, and you take the piece home the same day.

Creative workshops KL you can book this week

The workshops page lists this week as 23–29 November. Three sessions have a date page. Saturday the studio is closed. There is no public workshop on Friday. Acrylic, glow, watercolour, candle making and chunky crochet have no date yet.

  • Wednesday 25 November, 7pm–9pm — Sunny Bloom: Impasto Sunflower. Textured and impasto canvas, RM89, or RM160 for a pair. Two hours in the L2 front room. One canvas, built with texture paste or thick impasto paint and palette knives, on the sunflower theme.
  • Thursday 26 November, 7:30pm–9:30pm — Golden Impasto Sky. The same format, RM89, or RM160 for a pair, in the L2 front room. The theme is a golden sky in thick paint.
  • Sunday 29 November, 3pm–5:30pm — Gingerbread Baking Workshop. Bake Lab at Savor Level 1, Sri Hartamas. RM160 a person, or RM310 for two people (save RM10). You bake a gingerbread man and decorate a gingerbread house.

You book the seat before you arrive, from the Book button on the date page. The theme is guided. You can also WhatsApp +60 10-277 9426 or email info@masterartist.co and name the session.

Sunday workshops do not start before 12pm. Gingerbread starts at 3pm. Hours are Sunday 10am–6pm, Monday to Thursday 10am–10pm, and Friday 10am–5pm. On Fridays there are no workshops after 12:30pm. Saturday is closed.

What is included, and how a seat is booked

You do not bring paints, a canvas, wax, yarn or ingredients. Beginners and people who already paint, crochet or bake book the same seat. There is one theme for the table, and each person makes their own.

You can add a meal at Savor downstairs, from RM25. An evening session has the meal before. A daytime session has it after. Sunny Bloom and Golden Impasto Sky are before. Gingerbread, at 3pm, is after. The studio WhatsApps you Savor's menu.

Arrive 15 minutes early. The studio is upstairs, above Savor, at 38-2, Jalan 28/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur. Seats are prepaid and non-refundable. We confirm on WhatsApp and send payment details there. A reschedule is free within 24 hours of booking. After that it is RM10 a person, with 48 hours' notice. Each session needs a minimum of six people. If a date does not reach six, we message you and offer another date.

These are Launch Season prices, until 30 April 2027. A public holiday session is 10% more, except Sunday 8 November. Everyone who comes gets a RM50 voucher for a trial class.

A team day, if you want the room for a group

A public seat is one place at the sessions above. A private group is booked separately, on the groups page. An art team day is RM1,200 for up to 10 people. A baking team day is RM1,700 for up to 15. A mixed team day, with two or three streams in parallel, is RM2,400 for up to 20. Each extra person is RM100. The price includes materials and a teacher for each stream.

Team days run from 6 people. Up to four rooms can run side by side, and everyone takes something home. A dinner and evening workshop is RM150 a person, from 10 people. Friday team days finish by 12:30pm. Send the package, the numbers and a date from the groups page.

Frequently asked questions

What creative workshops can I do in KL this week?

This week, 23 to 29 November, three creative workshops are on the calendar in Desa Sri Hartamas. Wednesday 25 November, 7pm to 9pm, is Sunny Bloom at RM89. Thursday 26 November, 7:30pm to 9:30pm, is Golden Impasto Sky, also RM89. Sunday 29 November, 3pm to 5:30pm, is the gingerbread baking workshop at RM160. Saturday is closed.

How much is a creative workshop in Sri Hartamas, and how long is it?

Most public workshops are two hours. This week, textured canvas is RM89, or RM160 for a pair, and gingerbread is RM160, or RM310 for two people. Acrylic and candle making are RM89, glow canvas is RM85, chunky crochet and Bake Lab are RM95, and watercolour is RM80. Those other formats have no date on the calendar yet.

What is included, and what do I take home?

The price includes a drink and all the materials. You do not bring paints, wax, yarn or ingredients. You spend the session on one guided piece and take it home the same day: a canvas, candles in jars, a small crochet bag, or a boxed bake. At the gingerbread workshop you bake a gingerbread man and decorate a gingerbread house.

Where are the workshops, and how do I book a seat?

The studio is upstairs, above Savor, at 38-2, Jalan 28/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur. Painting is in the L2 front room. Gingerbread is at Savor Level 1. Book on the date page, or WhatsApp +60 10-277 9426. Seats are prepaid. Please arrive 15 minutes early. Each session needs a minimum of six people.

Can a beginner book, or can we take the room for a team?

Yes. Beginners and people who already paint or bake book the same seat. A private team day starts from six people. Art is RM1,200 for up to 10, baking is RM1,700 for up to 15, and a mixed day is RM2,400 for up to 20. Each extra person is RM100. Materials and a teacher are included.

If you want a creative workshop in KL this week, book Sunny Bloom on 25 November, Golden Impasto Sky on 26 November, or the gingerbread workshop on 29 November. For any other format, start on the workshops page. For a private group, use the groups page.
