Art Workshop KL: Themes, Times & How to Book

18 Nov 2026Master ArtistArtWorkshopsAdults

An art workshop in KL at Master Artist is a two-hour guided painting session at the studio in Desa Sri Hartamas. You book a seat, follow one theme, and take a finished piece home the same day. Materials and a drink are included, and the session is taught for beginners as well as people who already paint.

Watercolour and acrylic canvas are RM69. Textured and impasto canvas is RM79. Every date is listed on the workshops page. Below is the painting calendar for this month, how an art workshop KL differs from a walk-in art jam, and how to book the seat.

A paint night, art jamming, or an art workshop KL

A paint night and art jamming are both searches for an evening of painting with other people. An art workshop KL is that evening. At Master Artist that evening is a booked workshop. The seat, the theme and the materials are settled before you arrive.

You reserve a place for a set start time. It is not a walk-in art jam, and a spare chair at the door is not a seat. The theme is guided, so you are not left to copy a picture on your own. The price of the seat covers the materials and a drink, and you take the painting home the same day.

Watercolour is RM69. Acrylic canvas is RM69. Textured and impasto canvas is RM79. The session is two hours in the L2 front room, upstairs above Savor. Art workshops are taught by working artists from the Master Artist team. Cheong Chui Yen is the art lead.

Painting workshops in KL this month

If you want a painting workshop in KL this month, these are the art dates you can book. There is no painting workshop on the calendar this weekend. Saturday the studio is closed. The next painting sessions are mid-week. Both are beginner-friendly, both are in the L2 front room, and you take the canvas home the same day.

  • Wednesday 25 November, 7pm–9pm — Sunny Bloom: Impasto Sunflower. Textured and impasto canvas, RM79. One canvas, built up with texture paste or thick impasto paint and palette knives, on the sunflower theme.
  • Thursday 26 November, 7:30pm–9:30pm — Golden Impasto Sky. The same textured format, RM79, for two hours. The theme is a golden sky in thick paint.

Use the Book button on the date page, or WhatsApp us if you want to ask about a seat first.

After these two evenings, the painting weeks move to Tuesdays. Watercolour runs on Tuesdays at 10am and at 3:30pm from 1 December. Textured canvas runs on Tuesdays at 7:30pm from 8 December, as well as the November dates above. Those are the standing times. The theme for a later Tuesday is added on the format page when that date is ready to book.

Acrylic canvas is RM69 for one brush-on canvas, 30x40cm, from a guided theme. It is the simplest format for a first visit. New dates are coming, and none are on sale yet, so there is no acrylic session to book today.

What each art workshop includes

The price includes every material. You do not bring paints, paper or a canvas. A drink is included. Wear something that can meet paint, arrive 15 minutes early, and spend the two hours on one piece. Beginners and people who already paint book the same seat. There is one theme for the table, and each person makes their own.

Art jamming near Mont Kiara and Desa Sri Hartamas

For art jamming near Mont Kiara or Desa Sri Hartamas, the studio is upstairs, above Savor, at 38-2, Jalan 28/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur. Hartamas is a short drive from Mont Kiara. Painting workshops use the L2 front room.

Seats are booked, not walk-in. Message us or use the Book button, and the place is held once it is paid. That is the difference from turning up and hoping a chair is free.

Hours are Sunday 10am–6pm, Monday to Thursday 10am–10pm, and Friday 10am–5pm. Saturday is closed. On Fridays there are no workshops after 12:30pm, so we do not run a Friday paint night. Tuesday watercolour at 10am and 3:30pm, and Tuesday textured canvas at 7:30pm, fall inside those hours.

The team is on the about page. A private birthday is a different booking. If you want the room for a birthday group, ask on the birthday page rather than taking one of the public seats above.

How to book a seat

For Sunny Bloom, open the 25 November page and use Book. For Golden Impasto Sky, open the 26 November page and use Book. For a Tuesday watercolour, start on the watercolour page. For a later textured evening, start on the textured canvas page. You can also WhatsApp +60 10-277 9426 or email info@masterartist.co and name the session you want.

We confirm on WhatsApp and send payment details there. Seats are prepaid and non-refundable. A reschedule is free within 24 hours of booking. After that it is RM10 a person, with 48 hours' notice. Each session needs a minimum of six people. If a date does not reach six, we message you and offer another date. Please arrive 15 minutes early. We are upstairs, above Savor. A public holiday session is 10% more, except Sunday 8 November.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an art workshop in KL cost?

An art workshop at Master Artist is RM69 for watercolour and for acrylic canvas, and RM79 for textured and impasto canvas. The price is for one person, for two hours in the L2 front room. Materials and a drink are included, and you take the piece home the same day. Seats are prepaid.

How is an art workshop KL different from a walk-in art jam?

An art workshop KL is a booked painting workshop, not a walk-in art jam. You reserve a seat for a set start time and a guided theme. Materials and a drink are included, and you take the painting home the same day. Watercolour and acrylic canvas are RM69. Textured and impasto canvas is RM79.

Which painting workshops are on in KL this month?

The painting dates you can book now are Wednesday 25 November, 7pm to 9pm, Sunny Bloom: Impasto Sunflower, and Thursday 26 November, 7:30pm to 9:30pm, Golden Impasto Sky. Both are textured canvas at RM79 in the L2 front room. There is no painting workshop on the calendar this weekend, and Saturday is closed.

Can I walk in for art jamming near Mont Kiara or Desa Sri Hartamas?

No. Seats are booked, not walk-in. The studio is upstairs, above Savor, at 38-2, Jalan 28/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur, a short drive from Mont Kiara. Arrive 15 minutes early. Saturday is closed, and there are no workshops after 12:30pm on Friday. Book on the date page or WhatsApp +60 10-277 9426.

What is included, and can a beginner come?

Yes. These art workshops are beginner-friendly, and people who already paint book the same seat. You do not bring paints, paper or a canvas. Materials and a drink are included. You spend two hours on one guided theme and take the piece home the same day. Watercolour is on cotton paper. Textured canvas uses palette knives.

If you want a painting workshop in KL this month, book Sunny Bloom on 25 November or Golden Impasto Sky on 26 November. From December, use the watercolour page or the textured canvas page and take the Tuesday that suits you. Message us on WhatsApp when you are ready and we will confirm the seat.
