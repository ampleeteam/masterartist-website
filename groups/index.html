Activities

Team building activities in KL

Team days are art, baking, or a mix of the two. Art is a painting workshop. Baking is Bake Lab. Each one is two hours. A drink and all materials are included, and you take the piece home the same day. The price is one public seat. A private group uses a package above.

If you searched for a sip and paint, a painting workshop is that kind of evening: one guided canvas, with a drink included.

  • Acrylic Canvas. One brush-on acrylic canvas, 30x40cm, from a guided theme. The easiest format for a first visit. RM89 a person, pair RM160. Up to 18 seats. 2 hours in the L2 front room.
  • Textured & Impasto Canvas. One canvas built up with texture paste or thick impasto paint and palette knives, from a guided theme. Beginner-friendly; take it home the same day. RM89 a person, pair RM160. Up to 18 seats. 2 hours in the L2 front room.
  • Glow-in-the-Dark Canvas. One canvas painted with glow-in-the-dark acrylics from a guided theme: it looks finished in daylight and lights up when the lights go off. RM85 a person, pair RM153. Up to 18 seats. 2 hours in the L2 front room.
  • Watercolour. One watercolour painting on cotton paper from a guided theme: botanicals, fruit, koi. Beginner-friendly; take it home the same day. RM80 a person, pair RM144. Up to 16 seats. 2 hours in the L2 front room.
  • Bake Lab. One bake, boxed to take home. A familiar bread most weeks. The price on each date is the price for that bake. RM95 a person, pair RM171. Up to 24 seats (20 for a specialty bake). 2 hours in the L1 kitchen.

Public workshops also include candle making and chunky crochet. You book these as a single seat, not as a team-day stream.

  • Candle Making. Pour, scent and decorate your own candles in gift-ready jars, from a seasonal theme. Everything boxed to take home. RM89 a person, pair RM160. Up to 16 seats. 2 hours.
  • Chunky Crochet. Learn the basic stitches with chunky yarn and a big hook, and leave with your own small bag. No experience needed. RM95 a person, pair RM171. Up to 14 seats. 2 hours in the L2 front room.

Public dates are on the workshops page. A team day is a separate booking, not a stack of public seat prices.

Team building in Malaysia

Team building in Malaysia, for a team that wants to stay in the city, is a day at this studio. Searching for team building near me? We are upstairs above Savor, at 38-2, Jalan 28/70A, Desa Sri Hartamas, 50480 Kuala Lumpur. Hartamas is a short drive from Mont Kiara.

Up to 4 rooms can run side by side: the L2 front room, L1 front room, L1 kitchen and L2 recording studio. Friday team days finish by 12:30pm. Saturday is closed. Sunday workshops do not start before 12pm.
